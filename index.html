Where America's Insured Dogs Live: Pet Insurance by State, 2025
Flagship study by Moe Kahok

Study summary

  • Only 5.99% of American dogs were insured in 2025 — about 5.23 million dogs.
  • California holds 18.1% of insured dogs and 19.9% of premium; the top 4 states hold 37.8%.
  • Massachusetts and Connecticut lead on the Insurance Index at 1.18; Texas is 6.1% of dogs and 5.6% of premium (index 0.92).
  • Oklahoma has the lowest index at 0.60.
  • Average annual premium was $836 in 2025, up 11.5% from $749 in 2024.

Data: NAPHIA, 2026 State of the Industry Report — calendar-year 2025 data.
